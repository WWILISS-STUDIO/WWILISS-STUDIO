# WWILISS.STUDIO

### Original cinematic electronic music

Dark, emotional and atmospheric music created after work in Northern Ireland.

Independent artist blending cinematic soundscapes, electronic production, deep bass and honest storytelling.

---

## About the project

WWILISS.STUDIO is an independent music project built around late nights, city lights, rain, long drives and the determination to keep creating even when nobody is watching.

The music combines cinematic electronic production with dark but hopeful emotion.
